# Release notes: mealprep.mcbflow.com

Newest first. The same notes appear in the app under What’s new (footer, Settings › About, More).

## 1.9 · 2026-10-17
- The More menu on phones lists Settings, the other pages, Help and What’s new, in the same order as every mcbflow.com app.
- What’s new opens in one small window from the footer, Settings › About, More and Search. It replaces the long release history on the Help page.
- Help is reorganised: Getting started in four steps, then short answers grouped by topic, ending with Install the app and Your data & privacy. The full license moved to the Terms page.
- Search finds your recipes first (picking one opens it), then your plan, grocery list, ingredients, pages and help. Press / or Ctrl+K, or tap Search. The / key no longer jumps into the recipe filter.
- Demo recipes are now called Sample data. A short note at the top of Home has one button to remove it, with Undo. The welcome screen offers Start empty or Start with sample data.
- Deleting a recipe, a grocery item, a custom ingredient, leftovers, a past week or a day’s meals happens right away, with Undo in the message at the bottom. No more “Are you sure?” boxes.
- Clearer file names: mealprep-backup-2026-10-17.json, mealprep-recipes-2026-10-17.csv, mealprep-backup-2026-10-17.zip.
- Settings says “Changes save as you make them.” and has the same Install section as the other apps.
- Home banner: “Your week of meals”, with today’s date and how many meals are planned.
- Empty Recipes, Planner, Prep and Grocery pages say what to do next.
- Your recipes, plans, settings and backups are unchanged.

## 1.8 · 2026-10-17
- Clear all data now asks you to type DELETE, the same in every mcbflow.com app. The same box offers Save a backup first.
- Home: the Daily target, Recipes, This week and Grocery list tiles no longer show underlined text. They are still links: hover or tab to one to see it highlight.
- A very long recipe or grocery name no longer makes Home, a recipe page or Prep day scroll sideways on phones; it wraps instead.
- Account pop-up spacing fixed.
- The welcome banner art is no longer cut off on phones, and the banner keeps one height.
- Your recipes, plans, settings and backups are unchanged.

## 1.7 · 2026-10-16
- New Settings page (the gear at the bottom of the sidebar; on phones, More > Settings), in the same order as every mcbflow.com app: Appearance, Account & sync, Meal Prep settings (name, what you use the app for, meals, household, cooking style, servings, week start, prep day, units, budget, print ink), Backup & data, Install, About. It replaces the Data page, the About card and the sidebar's Setup button. "Run setup again" is in Settings, and the old About page is now Help. Old links (#/data, #/about, #/setup) still work.
- Account button in the top bar: "Sign in", or your profile picture with a sync dot when signed in. New "Keep me signed in on this device" choice (turn it off on a shared computer: the sign-in then ends when the browser closes).
- Profile picture and screen name: Edit profile in the account dialog (or Settings > Account & sync) lets you pick a screen name and one of 28 profile pictures, saved on your account so every app shows the same ones.
- Greeting: Home says "Good morning, <first name>" (your screen name when signed in, otherwise the name you gave in setup). The banner's own "Good morning" title is now "Your week of meals", so there is only one greeting.
- Home tab and gear: the first tab is Home with the suite's house icon, Settings uses a real toothed gear, Help a question mark, More three dots.
- The same welcome screen as the other apps: Welcome, Pick a look, Make it yours, Your targets (when macro targets are on) and Your kitchen, with Sign in in its header and "Sign in to sync" / "Import a backup" on the last step. The separate "Your data stays on this device" bar is folded into the first step (its saved flag is kept). Esc or Skip closes it.
- Clearer Data menu: every item has a one-line description, in the same order everywhere (Save backup now, Sync, Export JSON / CSV / ZIP, Import Backup, Clear all data), with the backup status underneath.
- New Export All (ZIP): the JSON backup, the full CSV (imports back) and one spreadsheet each for recipes, recipe ingredients, the meal plan, the grocery list, leftovers, prices and past weeks, plus a README. Import Backup accepts the ZIP too (it restores the JSON inside, with the same Merge or Replace choice).
- Quieter backup reminders while you are signed in and syncing: the backup chip reads "Synced" and never turns amber, the "Your data lives in this browser" banner and the "Save a backup file" step in Getting started are hidden, and the Data menu says "Synced to your account".
- Charts: the Planner's calorie bars, the macro bars and the macro rings show a label on hover, tap or keyboard focus (value, then day or macro, then context such as the difference from your target), the other marks fade, gridlines fall on round numbers, and the target line is labelled.
- Even top bar: every control is the same height, including the light/dark switch. New name style "Meal Prep ∙ mcbflow.com" (phones show "Meal Prep").
- One footer on every page: version, What's new, Privacy, Terms, More free apps, copyright and "Not medical or nutrition advice."
- Photos and files now open inside the mcbflow.com apps instead of downloading; Meal Prep has no photos or attachments, so nothing changes here.
- New backups say "mealprep-backup" inside. Backups from earlier versions still import.
- Privacy Policy updated for the ZIP export, profile picture and screen name, and "Keep me signed in".

## BETA 4.3 · October 9, 2026 · Smarter planning
- Browser tabs now read "Meal Prep | page name", for example "Meal Prep | Grocery".
- Auto-plan is smarter: choose which days to fill (skip days you eat out), set a longest cooking time and a weekly grocery budget. It leaves out recipes you gave a thumbs-down, favors thumbs-up recipes, and uses up leftovers first.
- Swap any single meal with one tap, without rebuilding the week. The swap checks the rest of that day so you stay near your targets.
- New week: saves the week you just finished to Past weeks, clears the shopping check marks, and lets you repeat the same meals, auto-plan a fresh week or start empty. Past weeks lets you reuse any saved week in one tap.
- New Prep page: your plan as one cooking session, with batches, containers, what goes in the fridge or freezer by day, ingredients to prep together, and a step-by-step checklist. Pick your prep day.
- Leftovers and freezer log: portions you already cooked are subtracted from the next grocery list, with "use soon" reminders for the fridge.
- Prices and costs: enter what you pay per unit once and see cost per serving and per gram of protein on each recipe, the estimated cost of the week, and the total for the grocery list. Sort recipes by cost, and plan to a weekly budget.
- Pantry check on the grocery list: tick everything you already have in one pass.
- Recipes get a thumbs-up or thumbs-down, and an optional total time in minutes.
- Backups carry all of it. CSV gains "rating" and "minutes" columns and "price", "stock" and "history" rows. Older CSV and JSON backups still import unchanged.
- Privacy Policy and Terms updated. Nothing about how data is handled changed: no accounts, no network connections.
